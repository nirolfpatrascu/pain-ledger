# Pain Ledger

Pain Ledger is a World of Warcraft Classic Era addon that keeps the books for **Painlocked**, a self-imposed Hardcore challenge, and writes a record of the run that anyone can check.

![Fate draws a card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nirolfpatrascu/pain-ledger/main/docs/images/01-fate-draws-a-card.jpg)

In one sentence, the challenge: you may only wear what rares drop and only spend what rares drop, you are locked in a zone until every rare in it is dead and its toll is paid, you can never go back, and Fate decides which gear slots and abilities you may use.

Pain Ledger does the bookkeeping for all of that, shows it on screen at all times, and seals every event in a hash chain so a run can be verified outside the game.
